How to label

Numbers show the order the page should be read aloud. They start as the XY-cut guess.

If the guess is right: just press Enter.

If it is wrong: press C to clear, then click the boxes in reading order (1, 2, 3 …).

Click a numbered box to take it out of the order (later numbers move up). Click it again to put it at the end.

Right-click a box to switch it between read and skip.

Rules

  • Columns: whole left column, then the next column.
  • A heading comes before the text under it.
  • Figure → its caption. Table → its caption.
  • Question → its options.
  • Sidebar / footnote: after the main text.
  • Headers, footers, page numbers, ads: skipped (grey dashed).
